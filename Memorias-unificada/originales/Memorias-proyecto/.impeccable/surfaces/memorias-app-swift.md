# Surface brief: Memorias (app del adulto mayor, iPhone + iPad)

Mode: Operate. 5 tabs: Hoy, Calendario, Recuerdos, Familia, Contar (asistente IA).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The app is one mixtape j-card for a life: tracklist rows with right-aligned times, one "now" marker on the tape. Refuses the generic care-app arrangement (pastel cards, tiny icons, nested menus).
OWN-WORLD: Black cassette shell with vibrant ballpoint blue (ink) and one amber tape-stripe accent; in light mode the white inlay stock with deep blue ink. Flat fills, hairline inlay rules, hand-lettered captions (Bradley Hand) only for personal words (captions, quoted stories); SF Pro for everything functional.
STORY: The elder opens it, learns what day and moment it is, sees who is who, hears and adds stories, and trusts every tap can be undone.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Hoy. Huge weekday + date, part of day; below, today's tracklist with times right-aligned and an amber AHORA marker line between past and upcoming; persistent large 5-label bar at bottom; "Pedir" voice pill top-right on every screen.
FORM: cassette j-card (vernacular-ephemera-cassette-j-card), assigned candidate 3; seed:a6c4e26f97a0aa91. Translation: palette pinned black+blue by user; handwriting kept to captions for legibility.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
