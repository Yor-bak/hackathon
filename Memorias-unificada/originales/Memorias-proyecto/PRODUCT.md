# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Stack

Swift + SwiftUI, compiled in Xcode. iPhone and iPad (adaptive layouts). Confirmed by user.

## Users

Primary: older adults (adultos mayores) with mild to moderate memory loss who want to stay independent and connected. They use the app alone, often at home, on iPad or iPhone. Design for the moderate case; it covers the mild one.
Secondary: a family member / caregiver (cuidador) with a mirror app. They monitor the older adult's activity and add memories to the album remotely.

## Product Purpose

An interactive memory album that orients and connects the older adult: photo/story memories, a family tree, a "today" view, and a simple calendar with reminders. Theme: "Adultos mayores: Independientes, conectados y seguros". Success: the older adult opens it alone, knows what day it is, recognizes who is who, and feels calm, never lost.

## Positioning

A memory album that is also a daily-orientation tool, with an AI assistant that interviews the older adult about their life and turns the answers into album entries, and a caregiver mirror app that feeds and watches the same album.

## Operating Context

Elder app: 5 screens (album de recuerdos, arbol genealogico, dia en curso, calendario, asistente de historias). Caregiver app mirrors it with monitoring and memory upload. Interface language: Spanish (Mexico). Voice assistant narrates and executes app actions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One-action menus only: no menus inside menus.
- Reversible decisions: no data loss; undo / "recently deleted".
- Confirmation on action buttons (clear feedback after a tap).
- High-contrast UI; extra-large text (Dynamic Type including accessibility sizes).
- Voice assistant for app actions; reminders; calm tone.
- Connects to existing resources (Photos, Contacts, Calendar) without risk of losing data.
- AI assistant asks about the person's history and saves answers as stories/memories in the album.
- Undecided: backend, sync, AI provider, caregiver permissions model.

## Brand Commitments

Binding visual constraint from the user: palette of black and blue, vibrant (not dull) so it stays easy to see.

## Evidence on Hand

None: no photos, people, or stories exist yet. Any sample content must be labeled synthetic.

## Product Principles

1. One screen, one job: each screen offers a single clear action.
2. Orientation before features: always answer "what day is it, where am I, who is this".
3. Nothing is ever lost: every action is reversible and confirmed.
4. Calm over clever: no urgency, timers, or surprises.
5. The caregiver amplifies the elder; never replaces their agency.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Extra-large text, high contrast (aim WCAG AAA 7:1 for text), 60pt+ touch targets, VoiceOver and voice control, Reduce Motion honored, no reliance on color alone, memory-loss friendly (consistent positions, no hidden gestures).
